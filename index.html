💡 Shop Points Multipliers ▼

Win games to earn shop points! Your earnings are multiplied by:

  • Game Mode: Each mode has a different multiplier (0.8x to 5.0x)
  • AI Difficulty: Harder AI = more pts (0.5x to 1.5x)
  • Restrictions: Enable restrictions for bonus multipliers!
  • 💎 Double Points Power-up: Adds +2.0x to total
  • Total: All multipliers are added together!
Example: Win on Reverse Rules (5x) + Master AI (1.5x) + No Bonus (1.2x) + Double Points (+2x) = 7.7x total!
